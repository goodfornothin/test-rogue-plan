Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

Rogue Bachata is a bachata dance school in London, run by Oscar deRogue and Bo. For any question about your information, email roguebachata@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you fill in one of our Meta (Facebook or Instagram) forms or book a class, we may collect:

  • your first name
  • your phone or WhatsApp number
  • your email address, if you give it
  • your form answers: your dance experience, whether you are free on Wednesday evenings, and whether you prefer group classes or private lessons

How we use it

We use your information only to contact you about our classes. If you ticked the box to hear about future events, we will also let you know about those.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for 12 months, then delete it.

Sharing

We never sell your information.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Just email roguebachata@gmail.com.

To stop hearing from us, reply STOP to any message.

Cookies

Our website uses the Meta Pixel to measure how well our adverts work.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk.
