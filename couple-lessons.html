Frequently Asked Questions

From £70 an hour, batch discounts, both instructors teach couples — details below.

Who are these couple lessons for?

Two people who want to learn together — partners, friends, or anyone who prefers practising as a fixed pair. Structured tuition, not a social mixer.

How is this different from the Couples / Move as One page?

Sensual Couples sessions are our dedicated in-home sensual partner-dance experience. This page is structured couple dance lessons — technique and connection you can build session by session. See Move as One if you want that sensual product.

How much do couple lessons cost?

From £70 an hour. Discounts available on batch lessons — packages and multi-session blocks.

Do we need dance experience?

No. We tailor every session to your level and how you like to learn together.
