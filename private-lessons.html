Not about the steps

Learn on Your Own Terms

Weekly classes are one way in — private lessons are another. Whether you're a solo dancer wanting focused attention, a couple wanting to grow together, or two people preparing a first dance for your wedding, a private lesson is built entirely around you. It's not about nailing choreography — it's about learning to listen to your partner, exactly like every class we teach.

Four ways to learn privately

Private Lessons We Teach

Both Bo and Oscar teach privately, and both teach couples — pick the format that fits what you're working towards.

Solo or with a partner

Private Bachata Lessons

One-to-one bachata technique — lead, follow, connection, and musicality — at whatever pace suits you. Great for beginners who want a head start, or experienced dancers refining specifics.

Beyond bachata

Private Partner Dance Lessons

Lead-follow connection, frame, and floorcraft — the skills underneath any partner dance, not just bachata. Built around what you already dance and what you want to improve.

Grow together

Private Couple Dance Lessons

Sessions for two people learning together — no dance experience needed. If you're after our dedicated sensual partner dance experience for couples, see the Couples page.

Your first dance

Wedding Dance Lessons

We choreograph and teach your wedding first dance from scratch — built around your song, your comfort level, and the floor you'll actually have on the day. No experience needed, ever.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

The short version: no experience needed, both instructors teach privately and to couples, and price depends on what you need — details below.

Do you teach private bachata lessons?

Yes — private one-to-one bachata lessons with Bo or Oscar, for solo dancers or with a partner, at any experience level.

Do you teach private partner dance lessons more generally, not just bachata?

Yes — private partner dance lessons covering lead-follow connection, frame, and musicality, built around whatever style and goals you bring.

Do you teach private lessons for couples?

Yes — both Bo and Oscar teach private lessons to couples. For sensual partner dance sessions specifically, see our dedicated Couples page.

Can you teach us our wedding first dance?

Yes — wedding dance lessons are one of our most requested private sessions. We choreograph and teach a first dance built around your song, your comfort level, and how much floor you'll actually have on the day.

How much do private lessons cost?

It depends on lesson type, length and frequency, so we don't quote a fixed price here — get in touch and we'll talk through what fits and send you a price and structure.

Do we need any dance experience for a private lesson?

No experience needed. Every private lesson is tailored to your level, whether that's an absolute beginner couple learning a first dance or an experienced dancer refining technique.
